Biography

Research profile

Dr. Cheolhee Yoo leads SURF Lab at Pusan National University. His research focuses on remote sensing, urban climatology, urban carbon, GIS and remote sensing applications, geospatial artificial intelligence, and smart city science.

Before joining Pusan National University, he was a JPL Postdoc Fellow at NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, California Institute of Technology, and a Research Assistant Professor and Postdoctoral Fellow at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.
