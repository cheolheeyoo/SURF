Cheolhee Yoo, Ph.D.

유철희

Assistant Professor
Smart City Convergence Major
Pusan National University

Google Scholar↗Email→

I study how urban development interacts with climate and the environment. My research combines Earth observation, geospatial artificial intelligence, and urban climate science to map cities, assess heat risk, and investigate pathways toward urban sustainability.

I joined Pusan National University in March 2026. Previously, I was a JPL Postdoctoral Fellow at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory and a Research Assistant Professor at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. I received my Ph.D. in Environmental Science and Engineering from UNIST in 2022.

My work spans Local Climate Zone mapping, satellite-based temperature estimation, population-aware heat-risk assessment, industrial land and carbon emissions, and green infrastructure cooling.
