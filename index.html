Research vision

From satellite pixels to policy-ready urban intelligence.

SURF Lab develops Earth observation and GeoAI methods for urban climate, smart city analytics, urban carbon, and environmental risk assessment.

⌁

Urban heat and thermal risk

Mapping air temperature, land surface temperature, local climate zones, and population exposure to support heat-resilient urban planning.

Remote sensing data fusion
