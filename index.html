About SURF Lab

Urban futures observed from space and translated through AI.

SURF Lab, the Smart Urban Remote Sensing Futures Lab, studies how cities grow, heat, emit carbon, and adapt. Our work combines satellite remote sensing, geospatial artificial intelligence, urban climatology, and smart-city analytics.

The lab focuses on building decision-ready urban datasets and models that help planners, scientists, and communities understand spatial inequalities in heat exposure, urban form, industrial land expansion, and green-infrastructure cooling potential.

Research themes

Core research directions

01

Urban Morphology Mapping

Mapping local climate zones and urban form using satellite imagery, building data, and deep learning.

 02

Heat-Resilience Assessment

Connecting urban morphology, land surface temperature, and population exposure to quantify heat risk.

 03

Industrial Land & CO₂

Monitoring industrial land expansion and its relationship with economic growth and carbon emissions.

 04

Tree Cooling Potential

Identifying where green infrastructure can most effectively reduce urban heat exposure.
